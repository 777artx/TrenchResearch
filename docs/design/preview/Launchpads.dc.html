Method and known gaps

A real launch has at least ten different wallets, other than the creator, buying in its first hour. Market cap uses the launch's fixed supply. Buyer return compares the price 24 hours after launch with the average first-hour buy price.

PonsFamily survival and $1M counts are understated: 31 of the 55 coins that left their bonding curve had no price data available afterwards. TEHscreener counts 1,008 PonsFamily launches that day against 859 found on chain, so a second launch contract is being traced.

Research, not financial advice. Sources: on-chain data from Robinhood Chain's public RPC; GeckoTerminal; DexScreener; tehcscreener.com.
